# MCX CIRCULAR (Reminder)

**Date of Issue: 21-11-2025 — Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX)**

## Subject: Reminder – Timeline for implementation of "Safer participation of retail investors in Algorithmic trading"

In terms of the provisions of the Rules, Bye-Laws and Business Rules of the Exchange, and in continuation of SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/0000013 dated February 4, 2025 (doc 01), this is a reminder referencing MCX's own prior circulars on this subject:

- **MCX/CTCL/235/2025** dated May 06, 2025 — MCX's initial implementation circular (parallel to NSE's doc 04, issued the same week)
- **MCX/CTCL/364/2025** dated July 25, 2025 — Standard Operating Procedure for safer participation of retail investors in algorithmic trading
- **MCX/CTCL/504/2025** dated October 01, 2025 — Extension of timeline for implementation of the SEBI circular

In order to provide more time to stock brokers who are yet to carry out the required system changes, a glide path was provided in SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/132 dated September 30, 2025 (doc 03), which is required to be adhered to by stock brokers, with the following milestones:

Milestone 1 — Registration of retail algo products coming through API (in-house and through vendors) and a few algo strategies for retail algo with exchange shall be completed by 30th November 2025.

Milestone 2 — Stock broker shall participate in at least one mock session with the new functionality, complete in all aspects, by January 03, 2026. The calendar for mock sessions shall be specified by exchanges. Stock brokers shall provide evidence to exchanges regarding participation. If a broker has not participated by January 03, 2026, that fact shall be intimated to the exchange.

Stock brokers who fail to adhere to the above milestones shall be barred from onboarding new retail clients for API-based algo trading framework w.e.f. January 05, 2026.

The framework specified in SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/0000013 dated February 04, 2025, along with implementation standards and detailed operational modalities (issued by exchanges), will be applicable to all stock brokers with effect from April 01, 2026.

Trading Members and their respective constituents are requested to take note of the same.

For clarification or assistance, contact the exchange's designated CTCL/algo compliance contact as listed in the underlying MCX circulars.

### Note on Milestone Numbering

This MCX reminder restates the SEBI-level milestones from doc 03 for MCX's own membership specifically — it does not introduce different dates from the SEBI circular (unlike, say, an entity that sets its own stricter threshold). Compare its milestone list carefully against doc 03's: this version omits "Milestone 1" from doc 03 (the October 31, 2025 registration-application deadline, which had already passed by the time of this November 21 reminder) and renumbers accordingly — a good test of whether a system notices a milestone is missing rather than assuming the numbering lines up across documents.
