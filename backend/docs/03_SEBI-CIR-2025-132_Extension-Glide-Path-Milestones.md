# CIRCULAR — SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/132

**September 30, 2025**

To, All recognized Stock Exchanges; Stock Brokers through recognized Stock Exchanges

## Subject: Extension of timeline for implementation of SEBI Circular dated February 04, 2025 on 'Safer participation of retail investors in Algorithmic trading'

1. SEBI issued circular on "Safer participation of retail investors in Algorithmic trading" on February 04, 2025 (doc 01). The provisions of the circular were to come into effect from August 01, 2025. However, based on representation from stock brokers and algo vendors, the timeline for implementation of the circular was extended to October 01, 2025 vide circular dated July 29, 2025 (not included in this pack).

2. The detailed operational modalities for the algo framework were issued by exchanges on July 22, 2025. Based on representations received by exchanges from stock brokers and algo vendors, certain clarifications and modifications were issued by exchanges in the second fortnight of September 2025.

3. In order to ensure smooth implementation of the framework, SEBI has been engaging with exchanges, broker associations and algo vendors. It was informed that a majority of stock brokers require more time to carry out the necessary system changes.

4. Accordingly, it has been decided that stock brokers who are ready with the required systems shall go live w.e.f. October 01, 2025. Further, a glide path is being provided for stock brokers who are yet to carry out the required system changes, with the following milestones:

   Milestone 1 — Retail algo product coming through API (in-house and through vendors) and at least one algo strategy shall be applied for registration with exchange by 31st October 2025.

   Milestone 2 — Registration of retail algo products coming through API (in-house and through vendors) and a few algo strategies for retail algo with exchange shall be completed by 30th November 2025.

   Milestone 3 — Stock broker shall participate in at least one mock session with the new functionality, complete in all aspects, by January 03, 2026. The calendar for mock sessions shall be specified by exchanges. Stock brokers shall provide evidence to exchanges of participation. If a broker has not participated by January 03, 2026, that fact shall be intimated to the exchange.

5. Stock brokers who fail to adhere to the above milestones shall be barred from onboarding new retail clients for API-based algo trading framework w.e.f. January 05, 2026.

6. Stock exchanges shall monitor the compliance of stock brokers with the above milestones.

7. Stock brokers who are not ready to go live from October 01, 2025 are advised to provide information regarding the number of existing clients as on September 30, 2025 to exchanges, in the format specified by exchanges.

8. W.e.f. April 01, 2026, the algo framework specified in the circular dated February 04, 2025, along with implementation standards and detailed operational modalities (issued by exchanges), will be applicable for all stock brokers.

9. Stock Exchanges are hereby directed to: (9.1) bring the provisions of this circular to the notice of their members/participants and disseminate on their websites; (9.2) put in place appropriate systems and procedures to ensure compliance; (9.3) make necessary amendments to the relevant Bye-laws, Rules and Regulations.

10. This circular is issued in exercise of powers conferred under Section 11(1) of Chapter IV of the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992, read with Section 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Stock Brokers) Regulations, 1992.

11. This circular is available on SEBI website at www.sebi.gov.in under the category: 'Legal → Circulars'.
